## Question format

The approach follows the observed patterns documented in [Question design](QUESTION_DESIGN.md): paired facts, comparisons, relationship reasoning, exceptions, concise applications, and numbered-statement combinations. Historical questions and their answer keys are not copied, translated, or used as new answer keys.

| Format | Skeletal | Cardiovascular |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Recall | 10 | 40 |
| Pairing | 25 | 46 |
| Comparison | 69 | 62 |
| Relationship | 54 | 46 |
| Application | 101 | 76 |
| Exception | 11 | 13 |
| Combination | 30 | 17 |

Each item has one defensible answer and four distractors. Numbered statements retain numeric labels when options shuffle. Feedback states the anatomical distinction in at most 60 words. Essential, Important, and Standard indicate study priority, not a prediction of a future examination.

## Source handling and review

- Skeletal references use B13 volumes 1–4. Cardiovascular references use CV lectures I–III. Citations identify actual one-based PDF pages; the dialog also supplies printed pages where applicable.
- Image-heavy lecture slides were visually inspected. Their diagrams remain valid source evidence even when text extraction was empty.
- Both historical question books and the embedded CV exam slides were used to check originality and format. Repeated or overly similar targets were replaced during review. Shared anatomical facts remain necessary for course coverage.
- All 600 new items received a second content review, including each combination subset. Automated schema and text-overlap checks supplement this review; they do not establish factual accuracy or originality on their own.
- All 63 distinct external URLs in the final new banks were checked. Retired e-library links that returned unrelated material and inaccessible alternatives were removed. University teaching tables were also read critically where a table contained an obvious typo.
- The source books and course scans are not uploaded. The public site provides their filenames and page numbers alongside public external references.

## Explicit lecture corrections

- **sa073**: Vol.1 PDF p.49 contains contradictory wording. Ribs 11–12 have short costal cartilages but no sternal attachment.
- **sa117**: Vol.1 PDF p.79 misclassifies mastoid air cells as paranasal sinuses. Vol.1 p.96 correctly associates them with the middle ear.
- **sp048**: The lecture gives only a partial attachment description. The complete four-point attachment includes scaphoid and trapezium radially, and pisiform and hamate ulnarly.
- **sp073**: Vol.4 PDF p.11 incorrectly includes the ilium in the obturator-foramen boundary; the bony boundary is formed by the pubis and ischium.
- **sp079**: Vol.4 PDF p.13 misplaces the pubic tubercle medially. It is at the lateral end of the pubic crest, although it is the medial endpoint of the inguinal ligament.
- **sp116**: The lecture combines cruciate functions broadly. This item separates ACL restraint of anterior tibial translation from PCL restraint of posterior translation.
- **sp131**: Vol.4 PDF p.35 calls the groove a tendon attachment. It guides the flexor hallucis longus tendon, which continues to the distal hallux; it is not its insertion.
- **sp137**: Vol.4 PDF p.37 overstates that each cuneiform contacts only one metatarsal. Adjacent metatarsal contacts also occur; this item uses the unambiguous proximal relationships.
- **cv193**: CV III p14 places inferior gluteal under the posterior trunk. In the usual pattern it arises from the anterior division; superior gluteal arises from the posterior division.

Normal branching variants and variable venous patterns are qualified within stems and feedback. A typical diagram is not treated as a universal arrangement.

## Updating the banks

Questions live in the two system-specific data files. Preserve IDs when correcting facts. New sources must include an actual lecture page and a relevant external reference, and newly introduced source hosts need review in the validator. Validate data, review the changed answers, and check the site before publishing. The system tabs use a shared interface; source metadata and filter labels are defined separately from question content.
