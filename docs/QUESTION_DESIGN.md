# Exam-style question design

The Exam-style category contains newly written muscular-system questions. The two historical books guide the question structure and choice design; they are not a source of questions to reproduce or translate.

## Observed tendencies

| Tendency | Evidence in the question book | Corresponding answer discussion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evaluate several anatomical statements and identify an exception | B12 Q43–50, PDF pages 13–14; B11 Q52–60, PDF pages 26–27; B10 Q49–57, PDF pages 38–39 | Answer PDF pages 25–26 and 42–44 |
| Combine name, location, attachment, action, or innervation rather than testing a name alone | B12 Q44, PDF page 13; B11 Q55, PDF page 26; B07 Q31–35, PDF page 66 | Answer PDF pages 25 and 43; the explanations correct the mismatched component |
| Match two linked anatomical facts in one option | B08 Q22–23, PDF page 56 | Answer PDF pages 87–88 |
| Judge numbered statements and choose the valid combination | B08 Q26, PDF page 56; B11 Q41–42, PDF pages 22–23 | Answer PDF pages 88 and 40 |
| Relate a muscle to a movement, anatomical region, or functional consequence | Matching sections at PDF pages 100 and 138–141; muscle-linked lesion questions at PDF page 10 | Corresponding explanations distinguish actions and the structures responsible |

These are qualitative patterns observed across the supplied papers, not a forecast of future exam questions. The books do not provide a reliable basis for attributing a preference to a particular instructor.

## New-question rules

- Five choices with one defensible correct answer. No references to answer letters, because choices are shuffled.
- New scenarios, tested relationships, statement sets, and distractors. No translated or lightly reworded historical items.
- Muscles remain central. Nerves, compartments, and bony landmarks appear only when they explain a muscular attachment, action, or functional relationship.
- Negative stems use one clearly marked NOT, INCORRECT, or EXCEPT. Numbered statements appear separately from the question heading.
- Facts cite B13 Gross Anatomy volumes 5 or 6 and public university or professional anatomy references.
- The reference dialog separates factual lecture references from historical question-style references.
- Where a historical answer is disputed or imprecise, it does not determine the new answer key. For example, the answer book itself gives two alternatives for B11 Q57 (PDF page 44). New items are reviewed for a single valid answer.

Every item was reviewed by its author and a second reviewer. Originality review combines comparison of tested relationships and scenarios with automated text-overlap checks against both supplied books; an absence of matching text alone cannot establish originality.
